**Purpose:** Compare observed application behavior with published guidance. This document is not clinical advice and does not validate the application for patient care.

## Source and Search Method

Google search requests were rate-limited during research. DuckDuckGo was used to discover sources; original publisher pages were opened with the available web fetcher. Sources whose exact rule text could not be retrieved are explicitly marked pending and are not scored as source-backed. No search snippet alone is treated as a verified clinical rule.

The source-care mapping below is a test-design assumption, not a statement made by the sources. Confirm it with the municipal health officer before calculating a score:

- Guidance requiring urgent or in-hospital referral maps to expected minimum RED.
- “Send home with advice” permits GREEN or YELLOW; RED is recorded as over-triage, not a defect.
- A result below a source-backed expected minimum is under-triage and a defect.
- Cases marked `DESIGN CHOICE`, `PENDING PHYSICIAN`, `PENDING SOURCE`, or `BLOCKED` are excluded from sourced accuracy.

For every case, begin with a fresh page. Use `/assessment` for **For myself** and `/assessment/child` for **Assess someone**. Fill only values specified in the case. Record the exact result text, symptoms detected, recommendation, medicine guidance, response time, and console/network errors. Do not reinterpret the input after submission.
